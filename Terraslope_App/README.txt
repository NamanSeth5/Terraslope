TERRASLOPE - how to put it on the internet (free, no coding)

Files to upload: index.html, terrain.txt, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png
(Do NOT upload template.html or artifact_index.html. They are working copies.)

Option A - GitHub Pages (about 10 minutes)
1. Make a free account at github.com and click "New repository". Name it terraslope. Keep it Public.
2. Click "uploading an existing file", drag in the six files above, click "Commit changes".
3. Go to Settings > Pages. Under "Branch" pick main and folder / (root), then Save.
4. After a minute your site is at https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/terraslope/

Option B - Netlify Drop (about 2 minutes)
Open app.netlify.com/drop, drag the folder containing the six files onto the page. You get a link at once.

Installing as an app on Android
Open the link in Chrome, tap the three dots, choose "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app").
After the first visit the app opens without internet.

Notes
- "Use my location" works only on the hosted link (https), not inside shared claude.ai pages.
