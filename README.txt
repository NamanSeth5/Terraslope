# Terraslope

Terrain-aware crop advice for small hill farmers in Sikkim, India.

Pick a farm location (GPS, town list or map) and a soil type. The app shows how well ginger, large cardamom and maize suit the place, using height, slope, soil pH and weather. It works offline and has English and Nepali.

**Live app:** https://namanseth5.github.io/Terraslope/

## Data and sources
- Terrain: Copernicus 30 m elevation model (slope and direction calculated from it)
- Weather: NASA POWER daily data, 2023-2025
- Crop rules: ICAR Sikkim Centre papers and the Sikkim Department of Agriculture crop guides
- District lines: 2011 census boundaries, public GitHub collection (udit-001/india-maps-data)

## Limits
- Guide only. Check with your KVK or block office before big decisions.
- Slope limits and the 1,000 m maize sowing split are rules of thumb.
- The Nepali text is a draft and needs review by a Nepali speaker.
